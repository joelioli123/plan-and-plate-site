Common questions

How do I share recipes with my household?

Sign in with the same account on each phone. Everyone sees the same recipes, meal plan and grocery list.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password. We'll email you a code to set a new one.

How do recipe scans work?

Tap + on the Recipes tab and choose Scan a Photo, then photograph a recipe card, cookbook page or printout. Every account gets 5 free scans; you can buy 100 more in Account → Recipe scans. Scans are tied to your account, so they work on every phone signed in to it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all of its recipes, photos, meal plans and grocery items.
