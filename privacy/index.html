Plan & Plate Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Overview

Plan & Plate keeps your recipes, recipe photos, weekly meal plan and grocery list so you can use them on every phone signed in to your account. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and how to delete it.

What the app stores

Your account: the email address and password you sign up with. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.

Your content: the recipes, tags, meal plans and grocery items you create, and any photos you add to recipes.

Usage events: which features are used, such as "a recipe was scanned" or "a meal was planned", with counts like the number of ingredients. They never include what you wrote: no recipe titles, ingredients, notes, photos, grocery items or your email. They are linked to your account’s random ID so we can see how the app is used over time. You can turn this off in Account → Share anonymous usage data.

The app does not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and it contains no ads. Your data is never used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Photos

The app only uses the camera when you choose to take a recipe photo, and only reads a photo from your library when you pick one. That photo is uploaded to your account so it shows on your other phones. Photos are private to your account.

If you use Scan a Photo to read a recipe from a picture, the photos are sent to Anthropic, whose Claude AI reads the recipe from them. The scan photos are deleted from the app’s storage as soon as the recipe has been read; only the text you save is kept.

Where your data is kept

Data is stored with Neon, a database, sign-in and file-storage provider. The app’s server only returns the records and photos belonging to the signed-in account. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with anyone. Neon stores it on the app’s behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. Photos you scan are processed by Anthropic only to read the recipe. Usage events are stored with PostHog, an analytics provider, and used only to improve the app. Anyone you give your account’s email and password to can see and change that account’s data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Home → account button → Delete Account. This permanently deletes the account and all of its recipes, photos, meal plans and grocery items. Deleting a single recipe also deletes its photo.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at the same web address, with a new date. For questions, use the support link on the app’s App Store page.
